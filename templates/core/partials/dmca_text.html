3. Submitting a DMCA Takedown Notice

If you are a copyright owner, or an agent authorized to act on behalf of one, and you believe in good faith that any content hosted on or accessible through recipe.klopot.tech infringes your copyright, you may submit a written DMCA notice to our Designated Copyright Agent containing all of the following elements required by 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3):

  1. Physical or Electronic Signature: A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.
  2. Identification of the Copyrighted Work: Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed, or, if multiple copyrighted works on the Service are covered by a single notification, a representative list of such works.
  3. Identification of the Infringing Material: Identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing or to be the subject of infringing activity and that is to be removed or access to which is to be disabled, and information reasonably sufficient to permit us to locate the material (including specific URLs, recipe identifiers, or workspace links).
  4. Contact Information: Information reasonably sufficient to permit us to contact you, such as your full legal name, physical address, telephone number, and an electronic mail address.
  5. Good Faith Statement: A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. Statement Under Penalty of Perjury: A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.

5. Counter-Notification Procedure

If content you posted was removed or disabled following a DMCA takedown notice, and you believe that the content was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification (for example, you hold the requisite copyright, have a valid license, or your use constitutes non-infringing fair use), you may submit a written Counter-Notification to our Designated Copyright Agent pursuant to 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3).

Your Counter-Notification must include all of the following information:

  1. Physical or Electronic Signature: Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. Identification of Removed Material: Identification of the recipe, photo, or culinary content that was removed or to which access was disabled, and the specific URL or location where the material appeared before it was removed.
  3. Statement Under Penalty of Perjury: A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification of the material to be removed or disabled.
  4. Contact Information and Consent to Jurisdiction: Your name, physical address, and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the Federal District Court for the judicial district in which your address is located (or Denver County, Colorado, if your address is outside of the United States), and that you will accept service of process from the person who provided the original DMCA notification or an agent of such person.

Upon receipt of a valid counter-notification, we will promptly forward a complete copy to the original complaining party. Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(2)(C), we are statutorily required to restore the removed content in not less than 10, nor more than 14, business days following receipt of the counter-notice, unless our Designated Agent receives notice from the complaining party that they have filed an action seeking a court order to restrain you from engaging in infringing activity relating to the material on recipe.klopot.tech.

6. Repeat Infringer Policy (17 U.S.C. § 512(i)(1)(A))

In accordance with 17 U.S.C. § 512(i), recipe.klopot.tech maintains a strict repeat infringer policy. We track validated DMCA copyright notices associated with user accounts via our system:

  • Strike Tracking: Each validated DMCA infringement notice concerning content posted by a user account constitutes a copyright strike recorded on that user's profile.
  • Account Termination: Any user who accumulates three (3) copyright strikes will be designated a repeat infringer. The user's account, workspace memberships, and all recipe publishing or uploading privileges will be permanently terminated without refund or appeal.
  • Egregious Infringement: We reserve the right, in our sole discretion, to immediately suspend or terminate any user account upon a single instance of intentional, egregious, or widespread copyright infringement.

7. Culinary Works and Recipe Copyright Notes

Under United States copyright law (17 U.S.C. § 102(b)), copyright protection does not extend to mere lists of ingredients or standard functional culinary procedures. However, copyright protection does extend to substantial original literary expression, culinary headnotes, personal commentary, recipe curation, and original food photography. Users must never copy and paste full creative narratives, commercial culinary books, or third-party food imagery without authorization.
