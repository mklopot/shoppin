Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Overview and Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Shoppin ("the Service," "we," "us," or "our"), created and operated by Waldorf Wellington in an individual developer capacity ("Operator"), collects, uses, and safeguards information when you access or use our web application located at recipe.klopot.tech.

We are committed to operating a clean, privacy-respecting culinary productivity tool. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not run third-party advertising networks or tracking trackers.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information only as necessary to provide the features of the Service:

  • Google Account Information (Single Sign-On): When you choose to authenticate via Google OAuth 2.0, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. We use this information solely to establish and maintain your user account and workspaces.
  • User Content: Any recipes, ingredient quantities, dietary tags, notes, directions, meal plan schedules, and shopping lists that you create, import, or organize within the application.
  • Client Telemetry & Agreement Records: When you execute our Terms of Service, we record device and connection indicators (including your client IP address, browser user-agent, display resolution, timezone, and language) alongside the timestamp to preserve an audit record of legal assent.
  • Session and Essential Cookies: We store standard first-party session cookies required for user login, CSRF security verification, and workspace state.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to operate, maintain, and deliver the Service:

  • To authenticate you and grant secure access to your private workspaces;
  • To synchronize shopping lists and pantry statuses in real time across your devices;
  • To generate grocery shopping lists automatically from your selected meal plans and recipes;
  • To ensure security, prevent malicious activities, and verify terms compliance.

4. No Sale or Monetization of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information or user-generated culinary content to data brokers, marketers, or advertisers. We do not display third-party advertisements on the Service.

5. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

Shoppin's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not share Google user profile data with any external partners.

6. Third-Party Infrastructure and Services

We rely on trusted cloud infrastructure to securely run the Service:

  • Hosting: Cloud servers hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS Lightsail) located in the United States;
  • Authentication: Google Identity Services (OAuth 2.0);
  • Supermarket Aisle Data: Kroger Developer APIs for optional live grocery aisle lookups (no personal user identity is passed to Kroger).

7. Data Retention, Portability, and Deletion

You retain ownership of your culinary data. You may delete individual items, clear meal plans, or purge workspace contents at any time from within the application interface.

If you wish to permanently delete your account, authentication records, and all associated personal data from our servers, you may send a deletion request to support@recipe.klopot.tech. Requests will be executed promptly within thirty (30) days.

8. Contact Information

If you have questions, feedback, or data privacy requests concerning this Privacy Policy, please contact Waldorf Wellington at support@recipe.klopot.tech.
