Tutorial 1 of 7 Shopping List

Shopping list with real-time transitions

Tap items to mark them as bought or needed. When anyone in your workspace makes changes, the list updates in real time via Websockets.

Sorting by priority and store aisle

Items are automatically grouped by aisle so you can move smoothly through the store. Flagging an item with Priority elevates it to the top of its section.

Pantry check before you shop

Switch to Pantry Check before leaving home to audit your pantry. Mark items as Have to keep them in storage, or Need to add them to your shopping list.

Search and filter recipes

Filter recipes by cuisine, meal type, diet, and equipment. Use (+) to require tags or (-) to exclude ingredients. Recipes can be browsed without logging in.

Import from a URL or create a recipe

Paste a web URL from cooking websites to automatically import ingredients and instructions, or write custom dishes in the recipe editor.

Organize with workspaces

Workspaces separate lists for home, work, or different households. Share your workspace with family members or roommates to collaborate on shopping in real time.

Preset lists and recurring schedules

Save reusable lists for recurring routines. Configure automated recurrence schedules to reload preset items into your list on set days and times.
