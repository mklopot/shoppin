Tutorial 1 of 15 Shopping List

Shopping list with real-time transitions

Tap items as you buy them to mark them as bought (🛒 Got) or needed. When anyone in your workspace makes changes, the list updates in real time via WebSockets.

High Priority toggle on shopping list items

Flag urgent or essential items directly from any item row using the 🚩 Priority toggle, or when typing in the Add bar. Priority items display a crimson tag and sort to the top of their aisle.

Setting store location & populating aisles

Select your supermarket from the dropdown or click Near Me / Map to locate nearby stores. Setting a store automatically queries store directory data (when supported by the store) to populate exact aisle and bay locations for your list items.

Sorting by priority and store aisle

Items are automatically grouped by aisle so you can move smoothly through the store in perimeter order. Choosing Priority sort puts all priority items to the top of the entire list.

The "Got" section & 7-day clearing

Checked-off items move into the Got section. Items not linked to recipes or preset lists are automatically cleared after a default of 7 days to keep your active list fresh and uncluttered.

Pantry check & confirming needed items

Audit your pantry before you shop. Items needing verification begin unconfirmed. Tapping 'Need' confirms you need to buy it and moves it to your active shopping list; tapping 'Have' confirms you already have it at home.

Search and filter recipes

Filter recipes by cuisine, meal type, diet, and equipment. Use (+) to require tags or (-) to exclude ingredients. Recipes can be browsed without logging in.

All Cuisines filter pulldown

Cuisine is shown here as an example of how each filter dropdown works across meal types, cooking methods, and dietary tags. Tap any option to require it (+), or tap minus (-) to exclude specific ingredients or diets.

Import from a URL or create a recipe

Paste any recipe webpage URL (Allrecipes, Serious Eats, NYT Cooking, blogs, etc.) into the Webpage URL input to scrape ingredients and directions automatically, or write custom dishes in the recipe editor.

Weekly meal plans & adding dishes

Plan your meals for the week (e.g. Monday Dinner, Taco Tuesday). Type any recipe into a meal to add dishes and automatically send required ingredients to your shopping list.

Recipe ingredients & cooking readiness

Tap any dish to open its recipe and see ingredient readiness: green (●) dots indicate items already in your pantry or cart, while amber/gray dots show ingredients that still need to be bought before cooking.

Organize with workspaces

Workspaces separate lists for home, work, or different households. Share your workspace with family members or roommates to collaborate on shopping in real time.

Preset lists and reusable templates

Preset lists bundle reusable collections of groceries—like weekly staples, breakfasts, or party supplies. Load an entire bundle onto your shopping list with a single click.

Automated recurring schedules

Configure automated recurrence schedules to reload preset items into your list on set days and times (e.g. every 7 days) so essentials are always ready for your routine shopping trip.

Re-run the product tour anytime

You can re-launch this interactive tour anytime by clicking your avatar in the top right and selecting Product Tour.
